# Kiwi Weather feed

Forecast data for the Kiwi Weather iPhone app: a blind-tested blend of global weather models for
Queenstown and the Wakatipu basin, the West Coast, Fiordland and the Southern Lakes ski fields and passes.
Updated every few hours by the forecast engine.

- `wakatipu.json`: hourly forecasts for Wakatipu places, the next-3-hours rain call and the backtest accuracy.
- `region.json`: daily forecasts for the West Coast and Fiordland (with tested rain odds) and snow at ski fields and passes.

Data credits: model forecasts from [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/) (CC BY 4.0) and
[Yr by MET Norway](https://www.yr.no/) (CC BY 4.0); Queenstown Airport reports from the
[Iowa Environmental Mesonet](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/). The blended forecasts here are
shared under CC BY 4.0 too.
